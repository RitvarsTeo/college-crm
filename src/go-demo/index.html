Built-in checks

What could go wrong, and what the system does about it.

Gift scans counted apartThe gift QR has its own link, so gift visits never mix with emails or typed visits.
One person, one registrationA guest who registers from the email and from the gift is one person: matched by email address.
Company names written differentlyEach registration is matched to the company list; a name with no match is checked by hand before the count.
Branches under the mother companyRiga and Tallinn branches of one group count as one company and one piece.
Registrations after the countA set count date, plus spare pieces cut with the rest.
Ticket used twice or forwardedEach ticket checks in once; a second scan shows the time of the first.
Several people from one companyThe piece goes to the first person of that company at the door; the rest are told who has it.
Personal dataThe form links the Novikontas privacy policy; details are used to organise the Grand Opening.
